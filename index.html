Hermes Fellowship

A private, owner-operated AI assistant for personal productivity, research and document work. This information site explains its Google account connection. It does not provide access to the assistant or accept registrations.

The connection lets the account owner work with Gmail and files in Google Drive, Docs, Sheets and Slides, and look up contacts. Its permissions include reading and managing mail and files, sending requested mail, creating and editing documents, and reading contacts. Google Calendar is outside this connection.

Eight assistant profiles use the same approved Google account connection. Profile names do not represent separate people or independent Google accounts.
